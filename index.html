Froggi Care Privacy Policy

Published by P226 Industries · Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: Froggi Care keeps everything you enter on your own phone, encrypted. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no ads, and no analytics. We never see, collect, or sell anything you write about your child.

1. Who we are

Froggi Care (“the app”) is published by P226 Industries (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at froggicare@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

The app lets a parent or caregiver keep a private log. Anything you choose to enter is stored only on your device, including:

  • Names you give to children, and notes, dates, tags, and severity ratings for events;
  • Tasks, reminder times, points, and rewards;
  • Your settings, and a salted hash of your PIN or password and of your recovery answer if you turn on the app lock.

This data is encrypted on the device using AES-256. The encryption key is held in your phone’s secure storage. We do not have a copy of your data or your key.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect, receive, transmit, or sell your personal information or anything you log.
  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

4. Reminders

If you turn on a task reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are created and delivered by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. By default, reminder text is generic and does not include a child’s name or a task title, and you can change this in Settings.

5. Backups and exports you create

The app can create an encrypted backup file, a PDF report, or a CSV file. You choose when to create them and where to send them (for example email, cloud storage, or another app). Once you share a file, it is handled by the app or service you chose and is covered by that service’s policies, not ours. Backup files are protected by a passphrase you choose. If you lose the passphrase, we cannot recover the backup.

6. Purchases

Optional paid features (a monthly Plus subscription and single-report purchases) are processed entirely by Google Play. Google handles your payment details and subscription status under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment information.

7. Children

The app is intended for use by parents, foster parents, and other adult caregivers. It is not directed to children, and children should not use it to enter information about themselves. Because the information you enter about a child stays on your device and is never sent to us, we do not collect personal information from or about children.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries, children, tags, tasks, and rewards at any time. You can erase everything from the lock screen (“Erase everything and start over”), and uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Backup files and exports that you created and shared are separate copies and must be deleted by you wherever you saved them.

9. Security

We use device-level encryption, an optional PIN or password lock with limits on repeated wrong guesses, and an option to hide the app preview in your phone’s recent-apps view. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and anyone who knows your PIN and has your unlocked phone can see what is in the app. Please protect your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: froggicare@gmail.com.
